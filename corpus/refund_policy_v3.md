# FictionalBank Refund Policy v3

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

## Overview

FictionalBank is committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with all purchases. This policy outlines the terms and conditions for refunds on customer purchases.

## General Refund Terms

Full refunds are generally permitted within **30 days** of purchase, subject to listed exceptions below. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval.

### Timeline

- **Within 30 days:** Full refund eligible
- **31-45 days:** Partial refund (50%) or store credit eligibility review required
- **After 45 days:** Refund eligibility expires; customer service escalation required

## Refund Exceptions

The following categories are **not eligible** for refunds:

1. **Used Digital Goods** - Once downloaded and activated, digital content cannot be refunded
2. **Customized Orders** - Items created to customer specifications cannot be returned
3. **Clearance Items** - Marked as final sale at point of purchase
4. **Services** - Consultation, support, and service fees are non-refundable

## Refund Process

1. Customer initiates refund request through FictionalBank portal
2. Compliance review completed within 1 business day
3. Approval or denial communicated to customer
4. Approved refunds processed within 5-7 business days

## Escalation

Refund disputes exceeding ₹10,000 require manager approval. Requests outside the standard policy windows are subject to escalation review.
