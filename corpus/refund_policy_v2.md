# FictionalBank Refund Policy v2 (SUPERSEDED)

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2025  
**Superseded Date:** August 1, 2026

**⚠️ WARNING: This is an archived version. Use Refund Policy v3 for all current refund decisions.**

## Overview

FictionalBank is committed to ensuring customer satisfaction with all purchases.

## General Refund Terms

Full refunds are generally permitted within **90 days** of purchase, subject to listed exceptions below. Refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval.

### Timeline

- **Within 90 days:** Full refund eligible
- **91-120 days:** Store credit only
- **After 120 days:** No refund eligibility

## Refund Exceptions

The following categories are **not eligible** for refunds:

1. Used Digital Goods
2. Customized Orders
3. Clearance Items
4. Services
