# Digital Goods and Services Non-Refund Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

## Digital Goods Non-Refund Terms

Once a digital good has been accessed, downloaded, or activated by the customer, it cannot be refunded under any circumstances. This includes:

1. **Software licenses** - Once activated or downloaded
2. **Digital subscriptions** - After the first access period
3. **eBooks and audiobooks** - After download
4. **Media files** - Once streamed or downloaded

## Exceptions to Non-Refund Policy

Limited exceptions may be considered if:

- The digital product is defective or non-functional
- The customer was overcharged due to system error
- A rare technical issue prevents product access

All exceptions require escalation to FictionalBank management and documentation of the issue.

## Consumer Protection

FictionalBank complies with all applicable consumer protection laws. Customers in jurisdictions with mandatory refund windows may have additional rights outside this policy.
